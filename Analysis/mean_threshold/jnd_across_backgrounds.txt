Just noticeable difference across comparison background conditions
======================================================================

Subjects: subject_cherry, subject_apple, subject_grape, subject_orange, subject_peach, subject_pineapple
Conditions: condition_1, condition_2, condition_3
Observer by block cells: 18

The JND is the difference between the 76% and the 50% points of the fitted
psychometric function. A purely additive contextual gain leaves the width of
the psychometric function unchanged, so a JND that does not vary with the
comparison background is consistent with alpha close to one.

JND by comparison background (mean ± SEM over the observer by block cells):

  B_c - B_s = -2.6 cd/m²:  1.99 ± 0.19 cd/m²
  B_c - B_s = +0.0 cd/m²:  1.87 ± 0.16 cd/m²
  B_c - B_s = +2.6 cd/m²:  2.03 ± 0.15 cd/m²

Friedman test across the three background conditions: chi-squared(2) = 1.78, p = 0.411

Paired comparisons against the equal-background condition:
  B_c - B_s = -2.6 cd/m²:  t(17) = 1.25, p = 0.230
  B_c - B_s = +2.6 cd/m²:  t(17) = 1.56, p = 0.138

Note: this comparison constrains alpha only loosely, so it is reported as a
consistency check rather than as an independent estimate of alpha.
